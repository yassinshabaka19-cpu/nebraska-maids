How Much Does House Cleaning Cost in Austin TX? (2025 Pricing Guide)

Updated January 2025 · 5 min read

If you're searching for house cleaning in Austin TX, one of the first questions you probably have is: how much does it cost? Pricing varies based on several factors, but this guide will give you clear, realistic numbers for 2025.

Standard House Cleaning Cost in Austin TX

For a standard cleaning — which covers dusting, vacuuming, mopping, kitchen wipe-down, and bathroom sanitizing — you can expect to pay between $100 and $180 for most Austin homes. Larger homes or those with more bedrooms and bathrooms will be on the higher end.

Deep Cleaning Cost in Austin TX

Deep cleaning goes beyond the basics — it includes baseboards, door frames, window sills and tracks, tile and grout scrubbing, vents, and cleaning under furniture. Expect to pay $180 to $280 for a typical Austin home. If your home hasn't been professionally cleaned in a while, a deep clean is the best starting point.

Move Out Cleaning Cost in Austin TX

Move-out cleaning is the most thorough — designed to meet landlord inspection standards and help you get your full security deposit back. This includes everything in a deep clean plus inside all cabinets, wall stain removal, and appliance cleaning. Prices typically range from $200 to $350 depending on home size.

What Affects the Price?

  • Home size (square footage, number of bedrooms and bathrooms)
  • Condition of the home (first-time cleans cost more)
  • Frequency (recurring customers get discounted rates)
  • Add-ons (inside fridge, oven, laundry, etc.)
