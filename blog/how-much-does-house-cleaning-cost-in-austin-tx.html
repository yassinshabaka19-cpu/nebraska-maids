How Much Does House Cleaning Cost in Austin TX? (2025 Pricing Guide)

Updated January 2025

If you're wondering how much a house cleaner in Austin TX charges, you're not alone. House cleaning cost Austin TX varies by home size, frequency, and service type. This guide breaks down cleaning service prices Austin residents can expect in 2025 so you can budget and compare fairly.

What Affects House Cleaning Cost in Austin TX?

Square footage, number of bedrooms and bathrooms, condition of the home, and whether you need a one-time or recurring clean all influence how much is a house cleaner Austin TX. Larger homes and those that need extra attention (e.g. first-time or deep cleans) cost more. Recurring customers often get better per-visit rates than one-time bookings.

Typical Cleaning Service Prices Austin (2025)

  • Standard house cleaning Austin TX — Most 2–3 bedroom homes fall in the $100–$180 range per visit. 4+ bedroom homes often run $180–$280.
  • Deep cleaning — Deeper scrubbing, baseboards, and detail work usually run $180–$280 for a typical home. See our deep cleaning service in Austin TX for what’s included.
  • Move-out cleaning — End-of-lease cleans that meet landlord standards typically range from $200–$350 depending on size. Our move-out cleaning Austin TX service is built for deposit recovery.

Recurring vs One-Time: How Much Is a House Cleaner Austin TX?

Weekly or bi-weekly plans usually offer a lower per-clean rate than single cleans because the team knows your home and the job is maintenance rather than catch-up. Many Austin TX cleaning companies (including Austin House Cleaners) quote based on your actual home details, so the best way to know your cost is to get a custom quote.

What’s Usually Included in House Cleaning Cost Austin TX?

Reputable Austin TX cleaners include supplies (all-purpose cleaners, vacuums, mops, etc.) in the price. You should not have to provide products unless you prefer specific brands. Always confirm whether add-ons (inside oven, inside fridge, etc.) are included or cost extra so your final bill matches expectations.
