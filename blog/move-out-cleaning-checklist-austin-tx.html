Move Out Cleaning Checklist for Austin TX Renters (Get Your Deposit Back)

Updated January 2025

Leaving your Austin TX rental? A solid move out cleaning checklist Austin TX renters follow can make the difference between getting your full deposit back and losing money to deductions. This guide gives you practical end of lease cleaning tips Austin and a room-by-room list so you (or your cleaning crew) don’t miss anything landlords and property managers look for.

Why a Move Out Cleaning Checklist Austin TX Matters

Property managers in Austin TX often do a walk-through with a checklist. If floors are dirty, appliances have residue, or cabinets are left with items or grime, they may charge for cleaning or refuse to return the full deposit. Using a move out cleaning checklist Austin TX style (or hiring a pro who follows one) keeps you aligned with what they expect. Many Austin renters use a professional move-out cleaning service in Austin TX so the job is done to landlord standards.

Kitchen: End of Lease Cleaning Tips Austin

  • Clean inside and outside of all cabinets and drawers. Remove shelf liners and wipe shelves.
  • Clean stove top, oven interior (if required by lease), and microwave.
  • Wipe down refrigerator inside and out; defrost if needed. Leave fridge and freezer empty and propped open if required.
  • Sweep and mop floors; wipe baseboards. Take out trash and replace liner.

Bathrooms: Move Out Cleaning Checklist Austin TX

  • Scrub toilet, tub/shower, and sink. Remove soap scum and hard water buildup.
  • Wipe mirrors, counters, and cabinets. Empty medicine cabinet and wipe shelves.
  • Sweep and mop floors; wipe baseboards. Remove personal items and trash.

Bedrooms & Living Areas

Vacuum all carpets and mop hard floors. Dust ceiling fans, blinds, and window sills. Remove nails and patch holes per lease. Clean light fixtures and switches. Closets should be empty and swept or vacuumed. Walls should be free of marks; touch-up paint if allowed.

Final Walk-Through Tips

Do a final pass with your move out cleaning checklist Austin TX list and fix anything you missed. Take photos of each room after cleaning for your records. If you’d rather not do it yourself, book a professional move-out cleaning in Austin TX so the property is inspection-ready.
