Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

1. Introduction

Austin House Cleaners ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our website or services in Austin TX and surrounding areas.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect information you provide directly, including name, email address, phone number, service address, and any details you submit in quote or contact forms. We may also collect information automatically when you visit our website, such as IP address, browser type, and pages viewed.

3. Use of Information

We use your information to provide house cleaning services, send quotes, schedule appointments, communicate with you about your bookings, improve our services, and send relevant updates or offers if you have opted in. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

5. Cookies and Tracking

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to improve user experience and analyze site traffic. You can adjust your browser settings to limit or block cookies.

6. Third-Party Links

Our site may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites. We encourage you to read their privacy policies.

7. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us at info@austinhousecleaners.com or (512) 555-0100.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

9. Contact Us

For questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, contact Austin House Cleaners at info@austinhousecleaners.com or (512) 555-0100.
